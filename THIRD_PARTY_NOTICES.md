# Third-party material

## R2RML-F

`Tools/r2rml-master/` contains R2RML-F, written by Christophe Debruyne and
distributed under the MIT License. The upstream project is available at
<https://github.com/chrdebru/r2rml>. Its original license is retained at
`Tools/r2rml-master/LICENSE`.

The copy included here was adjusted for this project's CSV workflow by removing
the unused Oracle JDBC dependency and using Apache Derby 10.14.2.0 for the
upstream test suite. The R2RML-F source is marked as vendored code for GitHub
language statistics.

## Included research paper

`data/Inputs/A_Systematic_Survey_of_Data_Value_Models_Metrics_A.pdf` is:

> M. Bendechache, J. Attard, M. Ebiele and R. Brennan, "A Systematic Survey of
> Data Value: Models, Metrics, Applications and Research Challenges," IEEE
> Access, 2023. <https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2023.3315588>

The PDF identifies the work as licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 International License: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>.

Other source documents used in the thesis are not redistributed in this public
repository. Their citations and retrieval information are listed in
`data/Inputs/README.md`.
